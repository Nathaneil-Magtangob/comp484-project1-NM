Arknights is a free-to-play mobile game developed by Hypergryph which also publishes the game's Chinese (CN) server, using Simplified Chinese, in partnership with Bilibili. Outside of China, Arknights is published by Yostar for the Japanese (JP), Korean (KR), and English (EN) servers, which are collectively referred to as the Global server.

Arknights is notable for blending elements of both tactical role-playing games and tower defense games, a combination that has contributed to its growing popularity in mainland China and internationally following its global release. Arknights is strictly a single-player game, though it features limited-time multiplayer events that allow for player interaction.

Development

Arknights was developed by Hypergryph Network Technologies, a Shanghai-based game studio comprised mostly of former MICA Team (of Girls' Frontline fame) employees. Most notably, co-founder and producer Hai Mao was previously a UI designer and character artist for the former.

Project announcement

On 17 May 2017, Hai Mao made a recruitment post on Weibo to find a graphic designer for a new studio. The post didn't mention a name, but contained an early logo for Hypergryph.

On 14 August 2017, Hai Mao teased an announcement about a new project. The next day, the first Weibo post from the official Arknights account was released, simply announcing “#Arknights It's time to set sail” with a CG from the game's opening sequence. Hai Mao revealed that Arknights was the next stage of a personal project called 3.0. He shared 9 artworks and his vision for the upcoming game: a story of people who refuse to give up hope. On some of these announcement artworks, the text XANADA v3.0GM can be seen, rooting the project in Hai Mao's Xanadu artbook series, started in December 2010 with Selenographic Rotation: Outline of Divine K.I.A.s, designated Xanadu v1.0Ga.

From August 16th, 2017 until its official release in 2019, the Arknights Weibo account began posting its worldbuilding concept under the name "the Recorder", introducing some of the earliest factions like Penguin Logistics, Rhine Lab, and Karlan Trade.

On September 8th, 2017, the first concept PV, known as "PV1", was posted on its official Bilibili account, formally announcing the game's development.

Beta tests

The first closed beta test of Arknights, called Awakening Test (唤醒测试 (huàn xǐng cè shì ) ), took place from December 1st to December 4th, 2017.

The second closed beta test of Arknights, called Echo Test, took place from July 20th to August 12th, 2018.

The third and final beta test of Arknights, called Conclusion Test, took place from March 29th to April 8th, 2019. Unlike previous two beta tests, Conclusion Test is an open beta test.

Almost 7 years after its mobile release, Hypergryph announced beta test for Arknights PC Client in early February 2026 with its full release scheduled a month later in March 2026 for CN servers.

Global server

The Global server of Arknights is released by Shanghai Yostar Co. Ltd. in January 16, 2020; about 9 months after the original CN release. Global server currently consists of three regional servers: English (shortened as EN), Japanese and Korean (shortened as JP and KR) respectively. The PC client for Arknights Global servers is released simultaneously on August 13, 2026.
